Put your own 3D models here as .glb files (and optional .usdz for iPhone Quick Look).

Then add an entry to ../experiences.json, for example:
  "model": "models/my-product.glb"

Tips
- Keep each .glb under about 10 MB so it loads quickly on mobile data.
- Model units are meters: a 20 cm product should measure 0.2 in Blender.
- Free converters: Blender (File > Export > glTF 2.0), https://gltf.report to compress.
